# Campaign launch scope — r6

The library contains 23 email roles and one alternative to W2. Building 23 designs does not mean sending all 23 to each contact.

## Initial proposal: seven templates, two flows

| Flow | Sequence | Reader's job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Welcome | W1 immediately → W2 at day 2 → W3 at day 4 | Understand the standard → read evidence → evaluate the catalog |
| Cart | C1 at 1h → C2a or C2b at 24h → C3 at 48h | Restore selection → review information/eligibility → resolve a question |

Choose W2 **or** W2-alt at the same step. They are content alternatives, not consecutive emails. C2a/C2b are mutually exclusive first-time/returning branches. A person receives at most three messages per sequence before exits, consent rules and frequency caps reduce that count.

## Later roles

W4 and C4 are optional engaged follow-ups. P1–P4 require payment/dispatch/delivery evidence appropriate to each step, with no duplicate transactional notifications. R1–R3 require meaningful engagement and are review invitations, not replenishment reminders. X1–X3 require an approved inactive segment and a working sunset action. H1 requires monitored support. B1–B3 are separately scheduled campaigns; B3 additionally needs an approved offer.

All roles have campaign-specific content and section maps in guide 16. The seven-template starting scope is a recommendation, not a confirmed conversion optimum. Use qualified clicks, assisted first orders, replies and complaint/unsubscribe rates to decide what to expand. Do not use open rate alone to infer research interest or consent.

## Global controls

One marketing message per 24 hours. Cart/support takes priority over welcome, then post-purchase, then repeat/win-back, then broadcasts. Recheck suppression and purchase state just before every send. Exit welcome on any first order. Suspend relevant marketing during unresolved support/refund/dispute cases. Coupon wording must match actual eligibility at the time of sending.

Local specifications and simulations are delivered; no live automation is enabled.

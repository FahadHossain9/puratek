# New Brand — 10 Image Request

Copy the prompt below into an image-capable chatbot. Fill every bracketed field and attach the new brand's real logo and product photos. The Puratek kit supplies the visual pattern; your assets supply the new brand identity.

```text
Create 10 separate campaign images for my new brand using the visual pattern documented in this guide:
https://github.com/FahadHossain9/puratek/blob/image-creation-kit/image-creation-kit/START-HERE.md

Repository: https://github.com/FahadHossain9/puratek
Branch: image-creation-kit
Kit folder: image-creation-kit/

NEW BRAND DETAILS
Brand name: [BRAND NAME]
Website: [WEBSITE URL]
Background color: [HEX COLOR]
Accent color: [HEX COLOR]
Products and quantities: [PRODUCT NAMES AND QUANTITIES]
Logo and actual product photographs: Attached.

Treat this message as the current brand configuration and campaign brief. Override the kit's Puratek brand settings and example one-image brief.

Read START-HERE.md and original-puratek-prompts.json. Visually inspect the supplied style references and my attached brand assets.
Use the Puratek references ONLY for composition, lighting, prop styling and overall visual quality.
Use my attached product photos and logo for product identity.
Do not include Puratek branding, product names or label wording.
Preserve my products' actual shapes, packaging, colors and labels. Do not recolor actual packaging merely to match the scene palette.

Create these 10 scenes, adapted naturally to my products:
1. Products inside an open white presentation box.
2. Products beside blank documents and a magnifying lens.
3. Products on a white tray with blank reference cards.
4. A single product beside a white box and accent-color ribbon.
5. Products beside a white shopping bag.
6. Products inside a white shopping basket.
7. Products on a white display plinth with ribbon.
8. Products beside an open shipping carton.
9. A product beside a blank feedback card and pen.
10. Products in a presentation tray with note cards.

Keep a consistent premium photorealistic studio style across all images.
Use my brand palette, white supporting props, soft lighting and subtle reflections.
Landscape 3:2. No headlines, coupons, CTAs, discount badges or invented claims.

Generate 10 individual images, not a collage.
Show image 1 for my approval before generating the remaining nine.
After approval, use image 1 as a style reference for consistency.

Return PNG masters, 1200 x 800 JPG exports where supported, and the exact prompts used.
Name files using the new brand name, scene number and version. Package them in a ZIP if possible.
Check the results for accurate logos, labels, product count, packaging and consistent visual style. Report unresolved mismatches.

If essential brand assets are missing, ask for them.
If the guide or references are inaccessible, ask me to upload the kit ZIP.
Do not claim to have inspected inaccessible files or invent packaging, logos or product information.
If you have no image-generation tool, clearly state that limitation and provide the final prompts instead.
```

For custom campaigns, replace the 10-scene list before submitting. The default scenes suit product still life; adapt them to the new product type rather than forcing every product into a vial-shaped layout. Generation does not authorize website edits or publication.

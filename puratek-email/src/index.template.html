Before any email can send

  1. Upload puratek-logo-light@2x.png to /wp-content/uploads/email/ on puratekpeptides.com (the send files point there).
  2. Set Puratek LLC's mailing address in WooCommerce store settings (the footer uses FunnelKit's {{business_address}}).
  3. Publish a Privacy Policy page and put its URL in place of %%PRIVACY_URL%%.
  4. Swap every %%TOKEN%% for the FunnelKit merge tag from its picker (table at the bottom); cart items with product images off.
  5. Site fixes 1–9 from the Source of Truth doc, and the client's plugin security check, are cleared.
